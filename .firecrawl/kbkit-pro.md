## Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about KeyboardKit Pro, licensing, and features.

What is KeyboardKit Pro?

KeyboardKit is the free, core SDK. [KeyboardKit Pro](https://keyboardkit.com/pro) is a commercial add-on that unlocks [pro features](https://keyboardkit.com/features) that take your keyboard to the next level.

What features are included in KeyboardKit Pro?

KeyboardKit Pro unlocks many [pro features](https://keyboardkit.com/features), like autocomplete & predictions, 75+ locales, AI support, dictation, an emoji keyboard, in-keyboard typing, a full document reader, and much more.

Can I try KeyboardKit Pro before purchasing?

Yes! You can try all pro features for free with a [trial license](https://keyboardkit.com/pricing). The [KeyboardKit app](https://keyboardkit.com/app) also lets you try many core and pro features directly on your device without writing any code.

How do I purchase a license?

KeyboardKit’s [standard licenses](https://keyboardkit.com/pricing) are puchased and managed via Gumroad, while [business licenseses](https://keyboardkit.com/business) can be purchased using any payment method you prefer, e.g. regular invoicing and bank transfer.

What is the difference between a standard and a business license?

KeyboardKit’s [standard license subscriptions](https://keyboardkit.com/pricing) are aimed at indie developers and startups, while a [business license](https://keyboardkit.com/business) is required for large companies and high grossing apps.

How are licenses validated?

KeyboardKit’s [standard licenses](https://keyboardkit.com/pricing) validate over a network call, and therefore require Full Access. [Business licenseses](https://keyboardkit.com/business) are validated on device, and do not need Full Access. Read more [here](https://keyboardkit.com/pro/license-handling).

Do licenses auto-renew?

KeyboardKit’s [standard licenses](https://keyboardkit.com/pricing) automatically renew until you cancel it from your Gumroad purchase. A [business license](https://keyboardkit.com/business) can stipulate its license renewal in its license terms.

Can I use one license for multiple apps?

KeyboardKit’s [standard licenses](https://keyboardkit.com/pricing) only cover a single app, while a [business license](https://keyboardkit.com/business) can be used for multiple apps within the same company.
